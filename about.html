Our story

About us

A family-owned protein shake & energy drink bar in South Reno, built around one simple idea: eating healthy shouldn't taste like a sacrifice.

Healthy,
but make it fun.

Crush Nutrition is a protein shake and energy drink bar with protein snacks, protein donuts and protein waffles, all under one very colorful roof.

We're a family-friendly spot with a focus on living a healthy, active lifestyle. That passion drives everything we pour: high-quality, delicious, nutritious options for our community, with a menu wide enough for every taste and dietary need. Gym-goers grab a Post Workout shake. Parents grab a Level 2. Kids grab a caffeine-free cooler (and usually a donut).

Low calorie. High protein. Clean ingredients. Come experience the blend of health and taste for yourself.
